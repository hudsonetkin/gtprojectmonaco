Reading your emails…

Chrome blocked the tabs. Chrome lets a site open only one tab per click until you allow pop-ups for it. This is a one-time setting:

  1. Click the Pop-ups blocked icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Choose Always allow pop-ups and redirects from this site, then Done.
  3. Click the button below.

No icon? Paste chrome://settings/content/popups into the address bar, click Add next to “Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects”, enter [*.]this site, then come back here.
