Building an electric boat at GT and racing it in Monaco

01The race

The Monaco Energy Boat Challenge

The Yacht Club de Monaco gives every Energy Class team identical catamaran hulls and the same 10 kWh energy limit. We compete in three events:

EnduranceMaximum distance in a four hour race
SlalomTight turns through Port Hercule.
SpeedHead to head one kilometer sprint

02The boat

Innovating on sustainable maritime technology

A multidisciplinary team of Georgia Tech students engineering propulsion, structure, energy, & telemetry systems. Scored on performance and innovation.

Propulsion
Motor & contra spinning propellers
Structure
Aerodynamic carbon fiber monocoque
Energy
Custom-built 10 kWh battery & solar
Telemetry
Sensing & live data analysis
Innovation
Present research in MEBC Tech Talks
